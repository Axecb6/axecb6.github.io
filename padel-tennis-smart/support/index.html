FourCourt (formerly Padel Tennis Smart) — by André Machado

FourCourt keeps one shared, live padel or tennis score on up to four Apple Watches. It runs on Apple Watch only.

Play with friends (group room)

  1. On one watch, tap Play with friends → Create room, choose the rules and note the 6-character code.
  2. On the other watches, tap Play with friends → Join with code, enter the code and choose a team.
  3. The host taps Start match. Any player can now tap a point or turn the Digital Crown; it appears on every watch a moment later.
  4. Each watch shows the score from its own team's side ("Us" and "Them"). Game and set wins and "Change ends" reminders arrive as a haptic on every wrist.

Group rooms need an internet connection (Wi-Fi, cellular, or through your paired iPhone). Group play is free.

Solo match and demo

To score on your own, tap Solo match, choose Padel or Tennis and tap each team's side to add points; the app handles games, sets, tiebreaks, golden point and super tiebreak. Solo matches work without internet. To see a four-watch room with only one watch, tap Try the group demo.

Room problems

  • "Room not found": check the code (letters and numbers only, no O, I, 0 or 1).
  • "This room has ended": the host closed the room, or it is older than 6 hours. Create a new one.
  • Score not updating: check that every watch has a connection. If it still does not update, the host can close the room and create a new one.
  • "Room is full (4 players)": a room has at most 4 watches.

Heart rate and Health

With your permission, the app shows your heart rate during the match and saves it as a workout in Apple Health. You can change this in the Watch app or the Health settings. Scoring works without Health access, and Health data is never shared in group rooms.

Premium

Premium is a one-time purchase (no subscription) that adds your full match history and win/loss record. Group play stays free. If you already bought Premium (for example after reinstalling the app), tap Unlock Premium and then Restore purchase; there is no extra charge.
