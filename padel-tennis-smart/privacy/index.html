Privacy Policy

FourCourt (formerly Padel Tennis Smart) — Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Versão em português

1. Who is responsible

FourCourt is an Apple Watch app developed by André Machado, an individual developer based in Portugal, who is the data controller for the processing described here. Contact: apps.support.cbaxe6@gmail.com.

In short: the App has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts or location. Solo matches, match history and Health data never leave your watch. Only the optional group rooms use the internet, with an anonymous identifier.

2. Match history (on your watch only)

The App stores your match history (scores, dates, duration and the generic player labels) locally on your Apple Watch. It is never sent to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

3. Health and heart rate (HealthKit, optional)

With your permission, the App reads your heart rate from Apple Health during a match and can save the match as a workout in Apple Health (duration and heart rate). This data is handled by Apple's HealthKit and stays on your devices and in your Health data under Apple's control. It is never included in group rooms, never sent to any server or third party, and never used for advertising. You can revoke Health access at any time in the Health settings; scoring works without it.

4. Group rooms (optional)

Solo scoring works offline. When you choose to create or join a group room, the watches in that room keep one shared score through Google Firebase.

  • Anonymous sign-in. The first time you use group play, the App signs your watch in to Firebase Authentication anonymously. This creates a random identifier, kept in your watch's Keychain. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or any other information about you. If you do not use group play for 30 days, this anonymous account is deleted automatically; a new one is created the next time you join a room.
  • What a room contains. The 6-character room code; when the room was created and started; for each player, the anonymous identifier, the team (A or B), a generic label ("Player 1" to "Player 4") and a last-seen time; and the current score and match rules. The App does not ask for or send names.
  • Where it is stored. In a Firebase Realtime Database in the European Union (europe-west1, Belgium), over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Who can access it. Only signed-in devices that know the room code can read a room; rooms cannot be listed. Each device can change only its own player entry, only players in the room can update the score, and only the host can create, start or delete the room.
  • How long it is kept. The host's watch deletes the room when the host closes it, and a guest's entry is removed when the guest leaves. The App ignores rooms older than 6 hours. If a watch loses its connection or the App is closed unexpectedly, a leftover room with the contents above may stay in the database until it is deleted; it contains no information that identifies you.
  • What is never sent. Your name, contacts, location, photos, Apple ID, health data or heart rate.

5. In-app purchase

FourCourt Premium is bought and processed by Apple through the App Store. The App checks your purchase with Apple's StoreKit on your device and stores the result locally. We never see your payment details, name or Apple ID. The App does not use any third-party purchase service.

6. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Group rooms: performance of the service you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Without the anonymous identifier and room data, the shared score cannot work.
  • Health data: processed only on your devices, with the permission you give in the system dialog, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Purchases: performance of the purchase contract, handled by Apple.

There is no profiling, no automated decision-making, no advertising and no analytics.

7. Service providers and international transfers

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC): anonymous sign-in and room database, as our processor under the Firebase Data Processing Terms. Room data is stored in the EU. Google may process the IP address of each request and may access data from outside the EU; such transfers are covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Apple: App Store purchases and HealthKit, under Apple's own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).

We do not sell or share data with anyone else.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To exercise them, email us. Because the App has no accounts and the room identifier is anonymous, we usually cannot link any data to you; most data can be removed directly by you:

  • leave or close a group room from the App;
  • delete match history by uninstalling the App;
  • revoke Health access in the Health settings.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Portugal this is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), www.cnpd.pt; you may also contact the authority of the EU country where you live.

9. Security

Room data travels over HTTPS and is protected by database rules that only allow the access described in section 4. The anonymous sign-in token is stored in the watch's Keychain.

10. Children

The App does not ask for any personal information and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the App changes. The date at the top shows the latest version, which is always available at this URL.
