Privacy Policy

FourCourt (formerly Padel Tennis Smart) — Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

FourCourt is an Apple Watch app developed by André Machado for scoring padel and tennis matches. This page explains what data the App handles and how. The App does not require an account. The App runs on Apple Watch only; an optional iPhone companion shows your match history.

2. Match History

The App stores your match history (scores, players, dates, duration) locally on your Apple Watch. If you use the iPhone companion, it receives a copy of that history from your watch through Apple's Watch Connectivity and keeps it on your iPhone. This data is never sent to our servers or any third party. Uninstalling the App removes the history stored on the watch and the companion's copy on the iPhone.

3. Health & Heart Rate (HealthKit)

With your permission, the App reads your heart rate from Apple Health during a match to show live stats, and can save the match as a workout in Apple Health (including duration and heart rate). This information is handled by Apple's HealthKit and stays on your Apple Watch and in your iCloud Health data under Apple's control. Heart rate and workout data are never included in group rooms and are not sent to any external server or third party. You can grant or revoke Health access at any time in the Watch/Health settings, and the App still works for scoring without it.

4. Voice Commands (optional)

The App lets you record points using voice commands, using your device's microphone and the operating system's speech recognition service (Apple). Audio is processed by the OS to convert speech to text — the App does not record, store, or send audio to any external server. This permission is optional; manual score entry works without it.

5. Group Rooms (optional)

Solo scoring works fully offline and never uses the network. Group play is optional: when you create or join a group room, devices in the same room keep the score in sync through Google Firebase.

  • Anonymous sign-in. The first time you use group play, the App signs your watch in to Firebase Authentication anonymously. This creates a random identifier stored on your watch. It is not linked to your name, email address, Apple ID or any other personal information, and it is used only so the database can check which device may write to a room. It is not used for advertising or tracking.
  • What a room contains. The 4-character room code; the room creation time and start time; the team (A or B) and a generic label for each player ("Player 1" to "Player 4"; the App does not ask for or send names); a last-seen timestamp used to show who is still connected; and the current match score (sets, games, serve, winner and the rules chosen for the match).
  • Where it is stored. In a Firebase Realtime Database in the European Union (europe-west1, Belgium). The App sends this data over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Who can access it. Only devices that signed in anonymously can read a room, and the database rules allow each device to change only its own player entry. Only the room's host can create or delete the room.
  • How long it is kept. The host's watch deletes the room when the host ends group play, and each guest's entry is removed when that guest leaves. The App does not show or join rooms older than 6 hours. If the App is closed unexpectedly or a watch loses its connection, the room may remain in the database with the contents listed above until it is removed. The App does not currently delete such leftover rooms automatically.
  • What is never sent. Your name, contacts, location, photos, Apple ID, health data or heart rate.

6. In-App Purchases

The Premium feature is purchased and processed directly by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. The App verifies your entitlement locally via Apple's transaction records and has no access to payment details (card numbers, etc.), which are handled entirely by Apple. The App stores the result of that check on your device. The App does not use any third-party purchase or subscription manager.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store — payment processing and purchase verification
  • Apple HealthKit — heart rate and workout data (on-device)
  • Apple speech recognition — voice commands, when enabled
  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) — anonymous sign-in and score sync for group rooms only. Google, as the operator of Firebase, may process the IP address of each request. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

The App does not use advertising, analytics, or behavioral tracking.

8. Your Choices

Match history and Health data stay on your devices, so you can delete them at any time by clearing the App's data, revoking Health access, or uninstalling the App. You can leave a group room at any time from the App. The App has no account, so there is nothing to close. Because the anonymous Firebase identifier is not linked to you, it cannot be matched to you; it is not removed automatically when you uninstall the App. For purchase-related data held by Apple, requests can be made through Apple's support channels.

9. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
