Privacy Policy

AI Evolution — Last updated: November 2026

1. Summary

AI Evolution does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything the App stores stays on your device.

2. What is stored on your device

  • Game progress — your virtual AI's name, personality, level, skills, recent game results and whether you have unlocked Premium. This is saved locally on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The App works fully offline.

3. Purchases

The optional Premium purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple ID, email or payment details. Apple only tells the App whether Premium has been purchased. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Third parties

The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services, and does not use the advertising identifier.

5. Deleting your data

Because your data only exists on your device, deleting the App deletes all of it. We hold no copy and therefore have nothing to delete on our side.

6. Children

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

8. Contact

Questions? Contact us at apps.support.cbaxe6@gmail.com.

Política de Privacidade

A AI Evolution não recolhe dados nenhuns. Não há contas, servidores, estatísticas, publicidade nem rastreio. Tudo o que a app guarda fica no teu telemóvel: o nome, a personalidade, o nível e as competências da tua IA, os resultados recentes e se o Premium está desbloqueado. A app funciona sem internet.

A compra opcional do Premium é feita pela Apple, na App Store. Nunca recebemos o teu nome, Apple ID, email ou dados de pagamento; a Apple apenas indica à app se o Premium foi comprado.

Como os dados só existem no teu aparelho, apagar a app apaga tudo. Dúvidas: apps.support.cbaxe6@gmail.com.
