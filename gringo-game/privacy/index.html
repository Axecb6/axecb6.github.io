Privacy Policy

Gringo: Card Game — Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Versão em português

1. Who is responsible

Gringo is developed by André Machado, an individual developer based in Portugal. Contact: apps.support.cbaxe6@gmail.com.

In short: we do not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics.

2. Playing on one phone or against the computer

In "One phone" and "VS computer" modes the game runs entirely on your device. The computer opponents are part of the App and run on your device; nothing is sent over the internet. The player names and characters you choose stay on the device and are discarded when the game ends.

3. Playing with friends nearby

When you play with friends, the iPhones in the same game connect directly to each other using Apple's Multipeer Connectivity (Bluetooth or a direct Wi-Fi link between the devices; no Wi-Fi network or internet is needed). The connection is encrypted, so other people nearby cannot read it. To play, each device sends the other players in the same game:

  • the name you type and the character you choose;
  • your "GRINGO!" voice recording, if you recorded one, so the other players hear it when you call Gringo;
  • the moves and the state of the game (cards, turns and scores).

This information only travels between the iPhones in the same game, during the game. It is never sent to us or to any server, and it is not kept after the game. The App asks for permission to use the local network the first time you play with friends; you can change this in your device settings.

4. Microphone and your voice

You can choose to record yourself shouting "GRINGO!" (up to 3 seconds). The App only uses the microphone when you tap the record button, and asks for permission first. The recording is kept only in the App's memory during that session and, when playing with friends, sent to the other iPhones in the same game as described above. It is never sent to us or to any server, and you can delete it or record it again at any time. Recording is optional: without it the App uses a built-in voice.

5. Purchases and statistics

Gringo Premium is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through your Apple ID; we never see your payment details. The App only stores on your device whether Premium is unlocked. Your game statistics (rounds played, rounds won, best round) and your chosen themes are also stored only on your device.

6. What we do not collect

  • No personal information such as email, phone number, contacts or location.
  • No account or registration.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.

8. Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
