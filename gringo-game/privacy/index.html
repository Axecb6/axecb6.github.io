Gringo: Card Game with Friends — Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Versão em português

1. Who is responsible

Gringo is developed by André Machado, an individual developer based in Portugal. Contact: apps.support.cbaxe6@gmail.com.

In short: we do not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics.

2. Playing on one phone

In "One phone" mode the game runs entirely on your device. The player names and characters you choose stay on the device and are discarded when the game ends.

3. Playing over Wi-Fi

In Wi-Fi mode, the phones in the same game connect directly to each other over your local network (Bonjour and a direct connection between the devices). To play, each device sends the other players in the same game:

  • the name you type and the character you choose;
  • the moves and the state of the game (cards, turns and scores).

This information only travels between the devices on your Wi-Fi network during the game. It is never sent to us or to any server, and it is not kept after the game. The App asks for permission to use the local network the first time you play over Wi-Fi; you can change this in your device settings.

4. What we do not collect

  • No personal information such as email, phone number, contacts or location.
  • No account or registration.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
