Privacy Policy

Manager Football Drills — Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Versão em português

1. Who is responsible

Manager Football Drills ("the App") is developed by André Machado, an individual developer based in Portugal. Contact: apps.support.cbaxe6@gmail.com.

In short: the App does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. Everything you create stays on your iPhone and your Apple Watch.

2. What the App stores, and where

The App stores on your device the drills, training plans, sessions, teams and preferences you create. If you add a squad, it stores the player names, shirt numbers and positions you type. This data:

  • is kept only on your iPhone, encrypted (AES-GCM) with a key held in the device's Keychain;
  • when you run a session, the information needed for it is sent directly to your paired Apple Watch through Apple's Watch Connectivity, so you can follow it on your wrist; it does not pass through our servers;
  • is never sent to us or to any third party;
  • is deleted when you delete it in the App or uninstall the App.

Like other app data, it may be included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud or computer), under Apple's control.

Player names. If you enter information about your players (who may be minors), you decide what to enter and are responsible for having the right to do so, for example under your club's rules. We recommend entering only what you need, such as a first name or a shirt number. We never receive this information.

3. Exporting and sharing (optional)

When you export or share a plan or drill, the App creates the file on your device and opens the system share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send it; we do not receive it.

4. Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions (including any free trial) are bought, processed and managed by Apple through the App Store. The App checks your subscription with Apple's StoreKit on your device. We never see your payment details, name or Apple ID, and the App does not use any third-party purchase service. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases: apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. GDPR

Because the App does not collect or receive any personal data, we hold no personal data about you. All data stays on devices you control, and you can view, change and delete it in the App at any time. The only provider involved is Apple, for the App Store and Watch Connectivity, under its own privacy policy. There is no profiling, no automated decision-making, no advertising and no analytics.

If you have a question about your data, email us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Portugal this is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), www.cnpd.pt; you may also contact the authority of the EU country where you live.

6. Children

The App is a tool for coaches and is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal information from anyone.

7. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the App changes. The date at the top shows the latest version, which is always available at this URL.
