Privacy Policy

Snooker Scoreboard — Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Versão em português

1. Who is responsible

Snooker Scoreboard is developed by André Machado, an individual developer based in Portugal, who is the data controller for the processing described here. Contact: apps.support.cbaxe6@gmail.com.

In short: the App has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Your matches, players and statistics stay on your device. The only data that leaves it is an anonymous identifier and purchase data, used to validate the Premium purchase.

2. Matches, players and statistics (on your device only)

The App stores your matches, the player names you type, scores, fouls, breaks, statistics, training sessions and settings locally on your device. This data is never sent to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the App deletes it. No account is required.

3. Voice commands (optional)

You can record points by voice. This uses your device's microphone and the operating system's speech recognition (Apple on iOS, Google on Android). Depending on your device and settings, Apple or Google may process the audio on their own servers to convert it to text, under their own privacy policies. The App itself does not record, store or send the audio, and only uses the resulting text to update the score. The microphone permission is optional; manual scoring works without it, and you can revoke it at any time in your device settings.

4. Sharing a result (optional)

When you tap Share, the App creates an image of the result on your device and opens the system share sheet. The image goes only where you choose to send it; we do not receive it.

5. In-app purchase (RevenueCat)

The Premium purchase is paid through the App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to validate the purchase and to restore it on a new device. For this, RevenueCat receives:

  • a random anonymous identifier created by the App (not linked to your name, email or Apple ID);
  • the purchase and transaction (receipt) data from Apple or Google;
  • technical data such as device model, operating system, App version, country and the IP address of the request.

We never see your payment details, name or Apple ID; payments are handled by Apple or Google.

6. App Store rating prompt

The App may occasionally show Apple's or Google's standard rating prompt. The prompt is handled by the operating system; the App does not receive your rating or review.

7. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Purchase validation and restore (RevenueCat): performance of the purchase contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), and our legal obligations for purchase records (Article 6(1)(c)).
  • Voice commands: performance of the feature you request, with the microphone and speech recognition permission you give, which you can withdraw at any time.

There is no profiling, no automated decision-making, no advertising and no analytics.

8. Retention

Data on your device is kept until you delete it or uninstall the App. RevenueCat keeps purchase data for as long as needed to validate and restore your purchase and to meet legal record-keeping obligations.

9. Service providers and international transfers

  • RevenueCat, Inc. (USA): purchase validation, as our processor under its Data Processing Agreement. Data is processed in the United States; the transfer is protected by appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework).
  • Apple / Google: payment processing, speech recognition and the rating prompt, under their own privacy policies (Apple, Google).

We do not sell or share data with anyone else.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To exercise them, email us. Your matches and statistics can be deleted directly by you, by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. For data held by RevenueCat, we can ask RevenueCat to delete the data linked to your purchase; because the identifier is anonymous, we may need you to tell us the date of the purchase to find it.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Portugal this is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), www.cnpd.pt; you may also contact the authority of the EU country where you live.

11. Children

The App does not ask for any personal information and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the App changes. The date at the top shows the latest version, which is always available at this URL.
